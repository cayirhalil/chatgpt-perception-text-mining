03 — METHODOLOGY

How It Works

Supervised sentiment classification meets unsupervised topic discovery, with a temporal layer on top.

Preprocessing

  • Removed null comments (1,467) and very short ones < 10 chars (20,486)
  • Dropped duplicate reviews (15,525)
  • spaCy en_core_web_sm for tokenization, stopwords & lemmatization
  • Emoji detection — 13.1% of reviews contained emojis

Sentiment Classification

  • Rating → label: 1–2 negative · 3 neutral · 4–5 positive
  • TF-IDF bigrams, max_features 10,000, min_df 3
  • Logistic Regression vs. Multinomial Naive Bayes
  • Class imbalance handled via undersampling

Topic Modeling

  • Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) + NMF
  • Evaluated with UMass coherence
  • 6 coherent themes identified
  • Per-topic mean rating links topics to satisfaction

Temporal Analysis

  • 9-month window: Nov 2023 – Jul 2024
  • Mean satisfaction declined as user base grew
  • Technical-complaint topics grew fastest
  • Consistent with AI-service scaling challenges
